# Gregory M. Newkirk, Ph.D.

Molecular biologist working where biotechnology, intellectual property, and software meet.

- Fourteen years at the bench across industrial strain engineering, nucleic-acid assay development, and NGS workflows (Cibus, BASF).
- Ph.D., Microbiology, UC Riverside: nanomaterial DNA delivery to chloroplasts. Papers in *Nature Nanotechnology* and *ACS Nano*.
- Inventor on U.S. Patent 11,186,845 B1.
- Founder of [Priori Intelligence](https://prioriintelligence.com): scientific and technical due diligence on biotech platforms, checking patent claims against the published evidence.

## What I build here

Tools for biotech IP and scientific evidence work: data pipelines over public regulatory and patent sources, and small interactive explainers that put real numbers in front of people.
